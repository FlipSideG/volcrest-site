✈️ Set up before your flight (offline audio)

1. Open this page once while online, then in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. The app and all verbs are saved on your phone.

2. For spoken Italian offline: Android Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech output → Google Speech Services → ⚙ → Install voice data → Italiano. This downloads a proper Italian voice that works in airplane mode.

3. On the plane: airplane mode on, then re-enable Bluetooth and connect your AirPods. Tap 🔊 or any conjugation row to hear it.

4. Test it now: go to Settings below and press "Test voice" with Wi-Fi off.
